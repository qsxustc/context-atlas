CONTEXT ATLAS

A map. A budget. A better prompt.

  1. Bring your code

    Open or drop a folder. Dependencies, generated files and common secret files are skipped. .gitignore rules are respected on a best-effort basis.

  2. Choose what matters

    Inspect the map, search files and build your selection. Auto-fit uses simple file priorities to stay within your estimated budget.

  3. Check, then share

    Review the entire Markdown or XML export before pasting into your AI tool. Detected credential patterns are redacted by default.

Files up to 1 MiB; projects up to 50 MiB of text. Token counts are approximate. Secret detection is heuristic and cannot catch everything. No files are sent to a server or stored after closing the page.
